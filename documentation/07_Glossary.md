# 07 — Glossary (Feature Terms)

## Nudge

A small, timely reminder when self-imposed deadlines are approaching or have passed. Nudges are linked to personal user settings/controls — no unnecessary reminders.

In the app, it appears as a small card on the dashboard with a primary button: "Do it / Mark done" and an optional "Swap" to get a different tiny step.

### Examples by Impact Measure

**Career/Work:**

- "Write 2 short bullet points for your most recent role."
- "Draft one 90‑second STAR story about a small win."

**Confidence & Self-Esteem:**

- "List 3 strengths and one example for each."
- "Write down one thing you did well this week."

**Skills & Learning:**

- "Spend 10 minutes on that course you started."
- "Read one article about a skill you want to develop."

**Wellbeing & Balance:**

- "Take a 5-minute walk outside."
- "Write 3 things you're grateful for today."

**Aspirations:**

- "Spend 5 minutes visualising your ideal future."
- "Write one sentence about where you want to be in 6 months."

---

## Weekly Check-in

The primary engagement loop. A weekly reflection that captures energy/mood and reviews progress since last check-in. Feeds into the baseline-to-current progress comparison.

---

## Weekly Challenge

A small, themed action sequence you accept and complete within a week, with a brief reflection at the end. Separate from the weekly check-in. Contributes to momentum.

---

## Momentum Days

A gentle alternative to "streak". Count of days you took any step in a period (e.g., this week). It celebrates momentum without pressure.

**Gentle logic:**

- Completing today's nudge increases momentum
- Missing a day pauses momentum but still shows your positive record (e.g., "best week")
- Optional grace rules: weekends don't break momentum; "4 days out of 7 keeps momentum"

**Microcopy examples:**

- "You kept your momentum yesterday — nice."
- "You showed up 3 days this week. That matters."
- "Missed a day? No worries. Try one tiny step today."

---

## Contextual Prompts

Template-based suggestions and check-ins that appear based on user progress, focus area, and momentum. No chat interface — users respond with preset options.

**Example:**

> "You're 80% through your CV goal. How are you feeling about your progress?"
>
> [Great!] [Stuck] [Need help]

Prompts are pulled from a curated library, not AI-generated.

---

## Swap

An optional action that replaces the current nudge with another tiny step if the first one doesn't fit today.

---

## Progress Ring

A visual indicator showing recent progress toward weekly wins or momentum days.

---

## Momentum Badge

The UI element that surfaces the current momentum signal (e.g., days active this week) without pressure.

---

## Tiny Step

A single, tiny, do-able suggestion for today. Think "one tile at a time," not "finish the wall."
