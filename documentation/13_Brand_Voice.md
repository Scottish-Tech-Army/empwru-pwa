# 13 - Brand Voice Update - "Voice for U"

**Date**: October 24, 2025  
**Status**: ✅ Complete  
**Brand Guide**: Voice for U — Warm · Honest · Modern · Human

---

## 🎯 Overview

Updated all customer-facing copy to match the new "Voice for U" brand guide. The voice is now warmer, more personal, and uses "U" throughout to reinforce the brand identity.

---

## 📝 Brand Voice Principles

### Essence

> "We sound like a trusted guide, not a guru."

### Tone Anchors

- **Warm** — Friendly and encouraging
- **Honest** — Straight talk with kindness
- **Grounded** — Practical and real-world
- **Confident** — Calm authority, no jargon
- **Playful** — Light, self-aware humour

### Key Language Choices

✅ **Use**: brilliant, fantastic, great, amazing, incredible  
❌ **Avoid**: awesome, fab, smashing, grand

✅ **Use**: take a breath, take a moment, stop for a minute  
❌ **Avoid**: pause, mindfulness, stillness, take a break

## ✅ Brand Voice Checklist Applied

All copy now passes the Writer's Checklist:

✓ Sounds like a real woman, not a brand  
✓ Honest but kind  
✓ Could say this to a friend  
✓ Guides, doesn't lecture  
✓ Leaves her feeling lighter or stronger
