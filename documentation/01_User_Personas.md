# 01 User Personas

The following personas have been developed to guide product design, feature prioritisation, and marketing strategy. They are based on an analysis of the target audience and enriched with up-to-date market research to ensure they reflect real-world user needs.

---

**Persona 1:** The Career Re-Launcher

**Name:** Leanne McKay

**Age:** 32

**Location:** Dalkeith, Midlothian

**Background:** A single mother of two young children (4 and 1), Leanne is a former retail assistant who recently completed a local employability programme. She is motivated but fears losing momentum without ongoing support.

**Goals:** Re-enter the workforce in a stable, term-time-friendly role (e.g., school admin) within six months; build interview confidence; learn basic office software.

**Pain Points:** Faces significant financial pressure from high childcare costs. Her time is fragmented into short, 5-10 minute bursts. She feels overwhelmed by complex goals and lacks confidence with formal CV and job application platforms.

**How empwrU Helps (Prototype)**

Leanne benefits from guided reflection exercises that help her clarify her "why" and break big goals into small, manageable steps. The baseline quiz identifies her starting point across impact measures, while visual progress markers and motivational nudges keep her on track during her fragmented 5-minute windows. Simple templates and contextual guides support her CV and application needs.

**Future:** Private community features for peer support.

**Persona 2:** The Job-Ready Seeker

**Name:** Kirsty Allan

**Age:** 27

**Location:** Paisley, Renfrewshire

**Background:** Currently unemployed and enrolled at Stage 3 of the Employability Pipeline via a local provider. Holds SQA Level 5 in English and Maths with recent barista and retail experience. Lives with parents, recently completed an employability short course, and is motivated to move into entry-level office or customer service work. Transport costs and repeated rejections have dented confidence.

**Goals:** Secure a stable 30–35 hour/week role within 8–10 weeks; refine CV and cover letter for customer service/admin roles; submit ten targeted applications per week and prepare for interviews; complete two short vocational modules (customer care and basic Excel); progress into Stage 4 (employment) and sustain at Stage 5.

**Pain Points:** Confidence dips after rejection; difficulty tailoring CVs to job descriptions; chaotic application tracking; interview nerves; anxiety with long ATS forms; travel costs for interviews.

**How empwrU Helps (Prototype)**

Kirsty uses the reflection-first onboarding to reconnect with her strengths and build resilience after setbacks. The impact measures baseline captures her starting confidence, and she can track her progress visually over time. Bite-size daily guidance keeps her focused without overwhelming her. Calendar reminders (via ICS export) help her stay organised with application deadlines.

**Future:** Application tracker, CV tailoring tools, interview practice features.

---

**Persona 3:** The Re-qualifying Professional

**Name:** Amira El-Sayed

**Age:** 28

**Location:** Leith, Edinburgh

**Background:** A former primary school teacher from Syria, Amira arrived in Scotland two years ago. She is currently improving her English (ESOL B2) and volunteering to gain local experience.

**Goals**: Secure a classroom assistant role within nine months; complete a Scottish Teaching Assistant certificate; build a network of UK professional references.

**Pain Points:** Her foreign qualifications are not directly recognised, creating a significant barrier. She feels socially isolated and anxious navigating formal UK application processes, preferring visual guides and simple language.

**How empwrU Helps (Prototype)**

Amira benefits from the clean, visual interface and simple, encouraging language throughout the app. The reflection-first approach helps her articulate her experience and transferable skills. Progress visualisation across impact measures shows her growth over time, building confidence. Contextual guides provide clear pathways for UK qualification recognition.

**Future:** Digital portfolio, buddy system, community features for peer support.
